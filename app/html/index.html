Listens to any event on this camera and sends it again under a short name, such as "Person Detected" instead of the long original.

Rules

Each rule listens to one event on this camera and sends it again under the name you give it. What came in and what was sent goes to the log (the Log button shows it). To copy the rules to other cameras, use Copy settings to other cameras on the Settings tab.
Adds Person Detected and Vehicle Detected rules, matched to this camera's Object Analytics scenarios. A rule with no matching scenario is added switched off, without a source event.
Steps a rule doesn't use are hidden. Show all steps draws them too, so one can be set up. Changes apply when you press Save.
